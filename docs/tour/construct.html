§7 — Build & fix

Align & Construct: build, combine and repair

Place one thing exactly against another, grow a face into a new solid, join or carve two objects, and rebuild a broken model from voxels, hollow or fatter. All of it inside the slicer, without going back to a CAD program, and the painting survives every step.

  • no gate
  • shipped
  • 2.4.8 · Remesh 2.4.9

The tool

Where Top toolbar›Align & Construct

Snap & Drag is for placing by hand. Align & Construct is for placing exactly, and since 2.4.8 it also builds: it extrudes a face into a new solid and runs booleans between two objects, or between two parts of the same object. It used to be called Align & Stack and it used to need Libre Mode. It does not any more: everyone has it.

JobWhat it does
Place #2Puts one object or part against another: next to it, on top, centred, or with an edge lined up.
Face of #1Pick a flat face of #1 and drop #2 on it.
Extrude from the faceTurns any flat face into a new solid, grown or shrunk around its outline.
BooleanUnion, Difference or Intersection of #1 with #2, without assembling them first.
Remesh (voxels)Rebuilds #1 as one closed solid: repairs it, grows or shrinks it, hollows it. The painting stays.

Picking #1 and #2, and parts

Click an object and it becomes #1, the one that stays. Click another and it is #2, the one that moves. Swap trades them. In an assembled object the first click takes the whole object; click it again and you get just the part under the mouse, and the rest of the assembly turns see through so the part stands out. #1 and #2 can be two parts of the same object. What you picked gets a layer of its colour: orange for #1, yellow for #2.

Place, and see where it lands

The pad is the bed seen from above: four arrows that follow the bed axes (red is X, green is Y) and a centre button. Three more buttons handle height: On top, Centre in height and To bed. Next to leaves #2 beside #1, touching it; Align edge lines up the edge on that side.

Hover any of those buttons and #2 shows up as a ghost where it would land, with a dotted line to a target mark. The line and the mark keep their size on screen, so a 3 mm part reads as easily as a big one. Click, and it lands exactly there.

Extrude from the face

Turn on Pick a face and click a flat face of #1. It lights up whole, holes included. Any flat face works, at any angle, and the new solid grows straight out of it.

  • Face extrudes the face as it is. Holes extrudes only its holes, filled: pick the flat face around a screw hole and you get the plug of that hole.
  • Height is how thick the new solid is, 5 mm to start. Offset grows its outline, or shrinks it with a negative value.
  • Both directions puts half the height out of the face and half into the model, so the new solid passes through the face. Handy as a cutter for a boolean.
  • Part keeps it inside the same object, in place. Object makes a separate one. Create does it, and one undo removes it.

A real use: a 0.05 mm sheet of white over the stickers of a keyring, to soften very opaque matte colours. It used to mean going back to the CAD program. Now it is a click on the top face. The new solid is a plain mesh, so if you change the model afterwards, make it again.

Boolean: #1 with #2

Union joins them, Difference carves the shape of #2 out of #1, and Intersection keeps only what they share. Two separate objects give a new object in the same place; two parts of the same object give a part of that object, in place, so the assembly stays together. With Keep #1 and #2 off, the result replaces both. If they do not overlap, or one is not a closed solid, it tells you and leaves the scene alone.

diagram Union, Difference, Intersection, and who gives the colour drag #2, pick an operation
A diagram in two dimensions. The real tool runs a mesh boolean on the 3D solids. The colour rules are the ones the app follows: the painting of both stays, unpainted skin keeps the filament of its own part, and the inside takes the filament of #1.

Since 2.4.8 the result keeps the painting, here and in the Mesh boolean tool and in Split › To Parts / To Objects. Every stroke stays where it was (colour, fuzzy skin, support and seam painting), and the skin you never painted keeps the filament of its own part. #1 gives the base filament, the one that fills the inside: in a Union, pick the big part as #1. In a Difference, the walls of the cut take the base filament.

On a big painted model it takes a few seconds, and a small window says Keeping the painting... while it works. Measured: a 218k triangle sea slug with all its skin painted merges in about 9 s and slices to the same G-code as the assembled one, 0.06 g apart. ColorStitch recipes, Texture Bump zones and stickers do not carry over yet.

Example: a looser hole for a screw

A downloaded cabinet handle whose screw holes come out too tight. No CAD needed: extrude a wider peg from the floor of the hole, then subtract it.

  1. Open Align & Construct and click the handle. It becomes #1.
  2. Turn on Pick a face and click the floor of the hole. The rest of the model turns see through, so you can see inside it.
  3. In Extrude from the face, set Height 25 and Offset 1, keep Part and press Create. A peg 1 mm wider all round appears inside the hole.
  4. Click the handle again to take just its body as #1, then click the peg: #2. Press Difference.
  5. The handle comes back as one part with a wider hole, in its place.
demo How much looser the hole gets move Offset and Height
A section through the handle. The arithmetic is the tool's: the peg's outline is the hole's outline grown by Offset on every side, and with Both directions half the Height goes into the model. The handle's shape is drawn, not loaded.

Remesh: repair, offset and hollow, with the painting

Orca's Fix model only exists on Windows, and the websites that repair a 3MF throw the painting away (and most of them charge for it). Remesh rebuilds #1 from voxels, tiny cubes, as one closed solid. Holes, loose open shells and faces turned inside out are gone, and with Keep painting on, the new mesh is painted like the old one. Every step happens on the voxels, with no mesh boolean, so it copes with meshes that are badly broken.

ControlWhat it does
QualityDraft 0.5 mm, Normal 0.2, High 0.1, Extreme 0.05, or any Voxel size from 0.02 to 2 mm. Smaller keeps more detail. Details thinner than one voxel are lost and sharp edges round off by half a voxel.
OffsetMoves the whole surface. Plus makes it fatter, so thin walls get printable; minus makes it thinner, room for a fit. 0 just repairs.
HollowTakes the inside away and leaves walls of Wall mm. Smooth fills the bumps of the inside, so it comes out like a bowl and not a copy of every tooth.
Open through the picked faceFace: the flat face you picked, less one wall all round, so a rim stays. Angle: spreads from where you clicked to every connected bit of skin that faces less than the angle away from it, for curved skins (flat areas open wide, tight curves stop soon, a rough mesh gives an organic edge); the green on the model shows what it takes. Circle: a round hole of Diameter where you clicked, like a drain hole. Without a picked face the hollow stays closed inside, and the panel says so.
Fast smoothBuilds the inside on coarser voxels. It is smoothed anyway, so it looks the same, and a big Smooth takes seconds instead of minutes.
Keep paintingPaints the new mesh like #1: colour, fuzzy skin, support and seam painting.
ResultReplace #1, a New object beside it, or Neg. part (below).
AdvancedFolded, the defaults work. Max triangles (the result is simplified down to this), Adaptive (bigger triangles where the surface is flat: at 0.1 a skull in Draft goes from 570k to 59k triangles and looks the same) and Remove loose bits (deletes tiny leftovers of hidden geometry, so Orca stops flagging the model).

A piggy bank out of anything, in two or three clicks. Pick a face, tick Hollow and Open through the picked face, choose Circle and a Diameter, and press Remesh. This skull came out hollow, with a 2 mm wall, a 40 mm round hole in the back and its painting kept. Coins go in, and come out, through the same hole.

A real repair. This painted skull bowl came out of a boolean with an eyeball, which lived hidden inside the model, poking through the wall. Remesh in High, Hollow off, Replace: about 10 s of voxel work, and the painting carried over in a few seconds more. The ball is gone; only a thin trace of it stays on the wall.

A bowl in one click. Pick the flat top of the skull with Pick a face, turn on Hollow, and the skull becomes a bowl with a rim of one wall, still painted.

Hollow without touching the model. With Result › Neg. part the inside, opening included, becomes a negative part of #1's object, and the slicer takes it away layer by layer. It takes about 2 s and works on any mesh, however broken. Move it, cut it, or delete it to undo. Happy with it? The Mesh boolean tool (Difference) makes it final, and the skull keeps its painting.

How long, on a painted skull of 850k triangles: Draft about 4 s, Normal with a hollow about 23 s, High with Smooth 10 about 24 s, painting included. While it works, a window lists every step of the run, ticks the ones done and has Cancel, which stops within a few seconds.

Changed from Align & StackNow
To bedDrops #2 only. It used to drop every object you had picked.
CentreCentres #2 on #1 in X and Y together.
On topLeaves #1 where it is. The "#1 to bed first" checkbox is gone.
Libre ModeNot needed. A whole object you lift up still drops to the bed without it, as everywhere else in Orca; a part moves inside its own object.
Meant to be used with Gravity

Align & Construct places the pieces exactly. Gravity is what then makes the slicer treat the touching face honestly instead of as a false bridge.

The painting survives every edit

Painting a model and then editing it used to throw the painting away. Now these keep every stroke (colour, fuzzy skin, support and seam painting), and skin you never painted keeps the filament of its own part:

EditSinceWhat happens to the painting
Boolean (here, Mesh boolean, Split)2.4.8Kept. The inside, and the walls of a cut, take the filament of #1.
Cut (plane, connectors, dowels, tongue and groove, Cut to parts)2.4.9Kept on every piece. The new face comes out with the filament of the part. A painted skull of a million triangles cuts in under half a second of painting work.
Remesh2.4.9Painted again from the old mesh, in a few seconds.
Fix model (Windows)2.4.9Kept. Not tested: the author only has a Mac.

ColorStitch recipes, Texture Bump zones and stickers do not carry over through a boolean yet.

The catch

  • The new solids are plain meshes. Change the model afterwards and you make them again.
  • A boolean needs two closed solids that overlap. If not, it tells you and leaves the scene alone. A broken mesh goes through Remesh first, or use Neg. part.
  • Remesh loses what is thinner than a voxel and rounds sharp edges by half a voxel. Pick a finer Quality for fine detail. Extreme on a big object takes minutes and gigabytes of memory.
  • A big Smooth only fills what is smaller than about twice its value. Large shapes of the inside (eye sockets, the nose) stay, rounded. A Smooth larger than the model is deep leaves nothing inside, and it tells you.
  • Remesh paints the new mesh from the old one. In Draft, borders between colours follow the bigger triangles and can look a little cracked; in the slice they come out clean. Big offsets blur the painting.
  • Circle drills straight in by its radius plus a wall. Where the model is thinner than that, it can come out on the other side.
